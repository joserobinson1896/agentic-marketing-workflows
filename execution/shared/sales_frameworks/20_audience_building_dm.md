# Audience-Building DM Framework

Source: client's own playbook, Step 2.

> **Loaded into generation**, alongside the Conversion DM, via the blend described in
> `15_warm_visitor_hybrid.md`. An identified website visitor doesn't need the segmentation
> this DM performs — the visit already did that — but its sharp personalized pain hook is
> exactly what a pure Conversion DM lacks. The four-step formula below is used for that
> hook, not as a whole message.

## Purpose

Extract a sub-audience of people in pain. This DM identifies who is relevant and who is not.
**It does not sell.** It segments a large network into a smaller group of potential buyers who
already suffer the pain you solve, so every future offer sent to them is relevant.

## Why it works

Simple, specific, pain-driven. If a lead doesn't relate to the pain, they won't respond — and that's
fine, they were never going to buy. If they do relate, they respond enthusiastically, and those are
the people who become buyers after a few more touches.

## The formula

1. Choose a pain (make more money / save money / live a better life / stay legal)
2. Choose a specific benefit
3. Choose a specific solution
4. Make it relevant to the prospect (personalization)

## Examples

> Hi Matthew, you got 11,831 followers on LinkedIn. I've identified 793 DTC founders with strong
> intent and assume 25 consider your Growth Marketing Services.
>
> There's a new way for Digital Marketing experts to secure those opportunities predictably.
> Can I share the walkthrough?

> Name, a few days ago I got your ad [keyword] and opted for [lead magnet]. Your emails (A,B,C) landed
> in spam and are likely costing you $2 per month per new subscriber.
>
> There's a new way for (NICHE) not just to get ALL your emails delivered but to decrease your cost
> per acquisition by (x). Can I share the walkthrough?

> Name, I've seen your ads and case studies. It seems like you're specifically targeting and working
> with Doctors.
>
> We've created a new fulfillment system specifically for medical that allows us to generate, convert
> & fulfill agency projects in less than 2 hours a week. Can I share the walkthrough?

## Notes

- Every "yes" builds the warm sub-audience. Every "no" or non-reply doesn't matter.
- Don't overthink. Results happen in the marketplace, not in your head. The best scripts come from
  sending and iterating, not from drafting.
