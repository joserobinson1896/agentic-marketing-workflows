# The Offer — Unified Dashboards

Edit this file to change what every generated email offers. It is loaded into generation
alongside the Conversion DM framework.

## What we sell

Done-for-you reporting. We connect the tools a company already runs into one dashboard,
build it in 14 days, and maintain it. There is no software to log into and no seats to buy —
this is a service, delivered.

## Who we're writing to

Someone who just visited the Unified Dashboards site after clicking an ad about a specific
reporting pain. They have already self-selected. Do not re-diagnose the pain from scratch;
they know they have it.

## The specific promise

One screen fed by their actual stack, live in 14 days, with no engineering time from their
side. Anchor on time recovered from manual reporting (the reference build went from ~11
hours a week to ~20 minutes) or on revenue their current reports cannot attribute.

## Ethical urgency and scarcity

Genuine delivery capacity, never a fake deadline. We onboard a small number of builds per
month because each one is hand-built. Phrase it as real capacity:
"I'm taking on 4 builds this month", "we've got room for 3 more starts before October".

## Price

Do not lead with price. This is not a chat sale — the framework's "lead with price" step
applies when someone can buy in the DM, and a $2.4k–$4.8k/mo done-for-you engagement can't
close that way. Price comes up on the walkthrough. If it fits naturally, "starts at $2,400
a month" is fine; never invent a discount.

## The call to action

Ask permission, low friction, one question. Good: "Want me to send over a sample dashboard
built on your stack?", "Can I show you what yours would look like?", "Do you have 20 minutes
this week or next?"

**Never** "start your free trial" or "sign up" — there is nothing to sign up for.

## Hard rules

- Name 2–3 of the tools we actually detected on their stack. Never guess at a tool.
- Never invent a metric, customer name, or case study that isn't in this file.
- Never claim we already looked inside their systems. We detect public signals from their
  website, and the message should be comfortable being read that way.
- No discount offers.
