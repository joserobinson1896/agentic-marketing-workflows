# Foundation — The Buying Ladder and the Two DM Types

Source: client's own playbook, Module 1. Loaded into every generation as context.

## The system

A daily client system that produces leads, customers and 4–5 figure sales directly in the DMs,
predictably, every week. It replaces randomness (referrals, luck, engagement spikes) with a machine.

It runs on **two types of DMs**:

- **Audience-Building DMs** — extract a sub-audience of people in pain. Not selling. Only identifying.
- **Conversion DMs** — convert that sub-audience into sales calls, buyers or clients.

## The 4 prospect stages

Every message has one job: move someone one step up this ladder.

1. **Lead** — someone you've earned the right to communicate with. They're in pain.
2. **Sales Lead** — someone you can sell to. Aware and interested.
3. **Buyer** — someone who has transacted.
4. **Client** — someone you commit time and resources to.

Mirrors: Attention → Interest → Transaction → Commitment.

## The 4 universal pains

Every offer, message or piece of content that converts ties back to one of these:

1. Make more money
2. Save money
3. Live a better life
4. Stay legal / stay compliant

## Philosophy — buyers vs leads

Leads don't pay bills. Buyers buy again. Don't obsess over getting attention — find the subset of
people ready to pay attention and turn them into buyers through simple, direct conversations.

Every sale, $100 or $10,000, comes from the same chain:

1. You find people in pain.
2. You present a solution they can believe in.
3. You give them a path to take action.

## How this project blends the two

Audience-Building DMs exist to *identify* who is in pain. An identified website visitor has
already done that work: they clicked an ad written about a specific pain and landed on the
page. The visit **is** the segmentation event, and they enter as a Lead on the ladder above.

So the pipeline does not run a pure Audience-Building DM — but it doesn't run a pure
Conversion DM either. A Conversion DM assumes the sub-audience was built through prior
conversation, so it opens straight on the offer; sent to someone who has never spoken to
us, that reads generic, and nothing in it proves we know anything about this specific
company.

The blend takes the Audience-Building DM's sharpest weapon — a specific, personalized
observation naming the prospect's pain in their own terms — and fuses it onto the
Conversion DM's offer and CTA. `15_warm_visitor_hybrid.md` specifies the structure.
Both `10_conversion_dm.md` and `20_audience_building_dm.md` are loaded into generation.
